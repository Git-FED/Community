# Age Data Retention

**Last updated: 2026-09-30**

> Not legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney.

Age-related data is limited to a self-attestation and client-side `localStorage` flag. It is not stored on the server, sold, shared, used for marketing, or used for profiling. A visitor can delete it by clearing site storage. Matrix IDs and membership records, if later requested through a separate access process, are governed by the privacy and membership terms.
