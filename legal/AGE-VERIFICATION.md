# Age Verification

**Last updated: 2026-09-30**

> This document is informational only and is not legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney in each relevant jurisdiction.

FED-OS is intended for people aged 18 and older. Entry uses a self-attestation checkbox and country selector; no identity document, birth date, or biometric data is requested. The attestation is stored client-side only and is not used for marketing, profiling, or disclosure to third parties.

Users discovered to be under 18 may be removed immediately under the community access terms. Age and jurisdiction requirements can vary by location, and this policy does not guarantee compliance with every local law, including COPPA, the UK Online Safety Act, GDPR, FADP, or PIPEDA.
