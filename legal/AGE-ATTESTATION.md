# Age Attestation

**Last updated: 2026-09-30**

> Not legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney.

The visitor confirms age 18+ and residence in a permitted jurisdiction. The website records a client-side approval flag in `localStorage`; no server-side record, document upload, ID, or birth date is required. Clearing browser storage revokes the local flag. Membership removal can also revoke access operationally.
