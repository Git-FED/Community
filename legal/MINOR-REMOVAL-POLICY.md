# Minor Removal Policy

**Last updated: 2026-09-30**

> Not legal advice. Consult a qualified attorney.

If a member is found to be under 18, administrators may remove the member immediately and revoke invitations. Paid amounts are not promised to be refundable where removal follows a false confirmation. Report suspected minors to `legal@fedpromptly.com` with the room, message link or screenshot, and relevant context. Administrators review reports and limit retained evidence to what is necessary for the decision.
